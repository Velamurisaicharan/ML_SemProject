MODEL EXPLANATION

Why Logistic Regression?

Logistic Regression is designed for classification problems. Instead of predicting the exact continuous Yield value, this implementation predicts whether a record belongs to the Low Yield or High Yield class.

P(High Yield) = 1 / (1 + e−z)

If the predicted probability is at least 0.50, the record is classified as High Yield. Otherwise, it is classified as Low Yield.
